Invisible Violin iOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026 · Developer: AN QI WEN

This policy describes the native Invisible Violin iPhone app. The separate browser-based violin experiment has its own technology and is not covered by this app policy.

Camera and hand tracking

The app uses the front camera only with your permission to locate hand positions and turn gestures into musical notes. Camera frames and hand landmarks are processed on your device using a bundled MediaPipe model. The app does not record, save or upload camera images or hand landmarks. Camera processing stops when you pause or leave the active app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your free-demo usage and whether you completed the motion guide in app preferences on your device. This information is not sent to the developer. The app does not require an account, use the microphone, include advertising or integrate an analytics service.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit service to obtain product prices and verify whether your Apple Account owns the unlimited-playing unlock. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Support and this website

If you contact us through the project’s GitHub support page, the information you choose to post is public and is handled by GitHub under GitHub’s privacy statement. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard request information such as IP addresses. Please do not include sensitive information in a public support issue.

Your choices

You can revoke camera permission in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its local preferences from the device; Apple retains purchase records according to its policies, so an existing purchase can be restored. No camera recordings are stored by the app or developer.

Contact and updates

For app support or privacy questions, contact AN QI WEN through Invisible Violin support. This page will be updated if the app’s data handling changes.
