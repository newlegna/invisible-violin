Invisible Violin for iPhone

Your hands. Four strings. An instrument you can play in the air.

Get started

  1. Place your iPhone upright on a stable stand in good light. Turn up the volume.
  2. Tap Start playing and allow camera access. Keep both hands visible and apart, with your palms facing the camera.
  3. Move the hand on the indicated screen side sideways like a bow to make sound. Use Swap bow hand if you prefer the other side.
  4. Raise or lower your bow hand to select E, A, D or G, from top to bottom.
  5. Bend fingers on your other hand to change notes. On D string, open, index, middle, ring and little finger play D, E, F, G and A. The highest held finger wins.

First finger not responding?

Keep your index fingertip and both knuckles visible. Tap Set index finger up, then show both hands and keep the index raised for two seconds. Bend it toward your palm and look for 1 DOWN. Move Index sensitivity to the right if you need an easier press. Raise your other fingers when testing the index.
